# TAAM Brand Kit

Paste this into a chat or a project's instructions. Anything made for TAAM (docs, posters, flyers, slides, social posts, pictures, web pages) follows these rules.

---

## 1. Who we are

- **Name:** TAAM (legal name TAAM Media LLC). Write it in caps: TAAM.
- **Tagline:** Making the Best Brands Better.
- **What we do:** the all-in-one growth partner for mid-size service businesses. Websites, content and search, run by one team so everything works together.
- **Founded:** 2025 by Theodore Allen (Founder/CEO, web and operations) and Alex Medina (Co-Founder, web marketing and media/content).
- **Where:** based in Las Vegas, works with clients nationwide. By appointment.
- **Website / email:** taambetter.com · info@taambetter.com

**Services**
- Website design and development: lead-gen sites with forms, booking and payments; logo and brand guides; on-page and off-page SEO; backlinks; email marketing; Google Business Profile setup; ongoing site management.
- Media and content: podcasts; short-form (Instagram, TikTok, X); long-form (YouTube, LinkedIn, company and training videos); photography.
- Systems: CRM setup.

**Who we sell to:** mid-size service businesses that have outgrown a single freelancer but aren't ready for an in-house marketing team. Their pain: three vendors, three invoices, a brand that looks different on every channel, and a website that doesn't bring in leads.

**The one idea every piece repeats:** one team, every channel. Site, content and search built to reinforce each other instead of being stitched together by vendors who never talk.

**Proof:** 145% increase in views for a client's content. Only use real results. Never invent stats, clients or testimonials.

## 2. Voice

**Bold, confident, strategic, modern, relentless.** We sound like a team that has already solved this problem for other businesses and knows exactly how to solve it for you.

- **Direct.** Say what we do and what it gets the client. Cut the filler.
- **Recommend, don't suggest.** "Here's what we'd do," not "you might consider."
- **Confident, not arrogant.** Back claims with results, not swagger.
- **Clear over clever.** Bold never means vague. No jargon.
- **Outcome first.** Talk about calls, bookings, leads and being found, not deliverables.
- **Short.** Short sentences, short lines. One idea per slide, poster or section.

**Phrases we use:** "Making the best brands better" · "One team, every channel" · "Built to perform" · Found / Called / Booked

**Words we never use**
- Agency filler: synergy, turnkey solutions, leverage, cutting-edge, best-in-class, solutions provider.
- Hedging: maybe, might, hopefully, we'll try, sort of.
- Anything that reads like a template.

**Example**
- ✗ "We offer turnkey digital solutions that may help you leverage your online presence."
- ✓ "Your site should ring your phone. We build the site, the content and the search that make it ring."

---

## 3. Pricing rule (never break)

- TAAM **never** gives discounts. Never write "discount", "% off", "deal", "save", "sale", "sale price", or show crossed-out prices.
- A smaller budget gets a **smaller scope at the same rates**, never a lower price.
- Bonuses are things we **add**, not money off.

## 4. Colors

| Role | Name | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | TAAM Orange | `#F46325` |
| Primary | Ink / Near Black | `#111111` |
| Neutral | Light Gray | `#F2F2F2` |
| Neutral | White | `#FFFFFF` |

**Orange tints** (backgrounds, charts, soft accents only): `#FF7B42` · `#FF9A66` · `#FFB38C` · `#FFD5C2`

**How to use them**
- Grounds are **orange or ink**. Alternate them strictly from one panel, slide or section to the next.
- Type on orange or ink is **white**. On white or light gray, type is ink with orange accents.
- Orange is the only accent color. No gold, blue, green or gradients.

---

## 5. Type

- **Montserrat only.** Headlines Montserrat Bold to Black (800–900), **ALL CAPS**, tight line height. Body Montserrat Regular, sentence case.
- **Outline type for statements:** a headline line set as an outline (white or orange stroke, no fill), paired with a solid line above or below it.
- **The orange marker:** one key word sits solid on an orange highlight block.
- Headlines are big. If a headline needs more than two lines, cut words.
- At most 3–4 short lines of body copy per poster, slide or card.

---

## 6. Imagery (strict)

**Only the TAAM character style.** Everywhere: slides, website, posters, social, docs.

- **The mascot:** the black-and-white rubber-hose cartoon character. Poses: `wave`, `peek`, `chill` (pointing), `phone`, `famous` (trophy), `counting`, `money-gun`, `sitting` (coin flip), `fishing`.
- **Props** drawn in the same style: bold black outlines, flat black and white, no shading. Existing props: phone-ringing, map-pin, search-bar, megaphone, stars, browser, growth, calendar, clapper, notify, coffee-cup, burst (starburst badge).
- **Supporting graphics:** the TAAM A-mark, halftone dot screen, doodle arrows, die-cut sticker treatment, big rounded image cards.

**Never**
- No photos, stock images, cinematic or 3D AI imagery.
- **No AI-generated humans of any kind:** no people, faces, bodies, hands, crowds or silhouettes.
- No other illustration styles (no flat-vector corporate people, no gradients, no clip art).

**Making new art:** generate with Codex (or any image tool) using the existing mascot PNGs as the reference. Every prompt ends with:

> flat black-and-white rubber-hose cartoon style, bold black outlines, no shading, white background, no people, no hands, no faces, no silhouettes

Check every output before using it. Reject anything with a person, face, hand or silhouette in it.

## 7. Layout direction

- **One idea per piece.** A big headline, at most 3–4 short lines, one CTA.
- **Editorial / sales look:** huge all-caps headline, lots of space, strong blocks of orange and ink.
- **Rounded cards:** images and tips sit on big rounded white cards.
- **Tilted bands and tickers** for energy: a marquee strip alternating solid and outline words.
- **Mascot as the guide:** it points at the key line, peeks over a card, or holds the prop that matches the message.
- Cut anything a reader would skim past.

**Format starters**
- **Poster / flyer:** orange or ink ground → outline + solid headline → one mascot pose → one line → CTA pill + taambetter.com.
- **Social post:** one word or number punch on the orange marker, mascot or prop, logo bottom corner.
- **Doc / proposal:** white pages, ink body text, orange section rules and headings in caps, one orange-or-ink cover page with the mascot.
- **Slide:** one idea, big headline, orange/ink alternating slide to slide.
